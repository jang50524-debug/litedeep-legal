1. Information We Collect

The Application may collect the following information when you use the Application:

Email address: Used for account registration, account management, and providing the Application's services.

Address: Used to provide location-related services and recommendations.

User preferences and selections: Information provided by users regarding their preferences may be used to provide personalized workation destination and itinerary recommendations.

Review information: When users submit reviews, the Application may collect ratings, selected locations, review content, and the date and time the review was submitted.

Review free-text content: Users may voluntarily provide written comments or other free-text content as part of their reviews.

Reviews submitted through the Application may be displayed to other users of the Application. Information displayed as part of a review will be limited to information intended to be publicly displayed through the review feature. The Service Provider will not intentionally display a user's email address or other account information as part of a publicly displayed review.

2. How We Use Your Information

The Service Provider may use collected information for the following purposes:

To create and manage user accounts

To provide and maintain the Application's services

To provide personalized workation destination and itinerary recommendations

To analyze user preferences and service usage

To process and manage user reviews

To display reviews and review-related information to other users

To improve the quality, functionality, and usability of the Application

To develop, test, and improve existing and future features and services

To improve recommendation and analysis features, including AI-powered features

To communicate with users regarding important service-related information

Use of Reviews and User-Generated Content

Reviews submitted by users may be used to provide information to other users and to improve the Application and develop future features and services.

Review information, including ratings, selected locations, and the date and time of submission, may be analyzed to understand user preferences and improve recommendation services.

Free-text content voluntarily submitted as part of a review may also be used for service improvement, feature development, recommendation improvement, and analysis of user feedback.

The Service Provider may use user-submitted review data for the development and improvement of the Application, including future versions, features, and recommendation services.

4. Data Retention

The Service Provider retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required by applicable law.

Account information may be retained while the user's account remains active.

Review information, including ratings, selected locations, and review submission dates, may be retained while the user's account remains active for the purposes of providing the review feature and improving the Application.

Retention of Free-Text Review Content

Free-text content submitted by users as part of reviews may be retained and used for service improvement and development while the user's account is active.

When a user deletes their account, the user's review free-text content will be deleted from the Service Provider's active systems, except where retention is required by applicable law.

Review-related structured information, such as ratings, selected locations, and review submission dates, may be retained separately where necessary for service improvement or analytical purposes and where permitted by applicable law.
